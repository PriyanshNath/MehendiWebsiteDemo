FAQs

Useful things to know before booking.

How early should I book bridal mehendi?

For peak wedding months, six to eight weeks is ideal. Short-notice dates can still be checked through WhatsApp.

Do you travel outside Guwahati?

Yes. Travel is available across Assam and nearby North East India cities depending on the date and event schedule.

Can the design include personal motifs?

Yes. Names, dates, pets, instruments, places and symbolic details can be folded into the bridal layout.

What helps the stain become darker?

Keep the paste on longer, avoid water immediately after removal, and follow the aftercare shared before your appointment.
